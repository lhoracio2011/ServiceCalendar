# Service Calendar

Service Calendar is a simple work log for people who work at several places. You keep a list of places and procedures, set a price for each procedure at each place, and record what you did on each day. The app adds up your earnings and builds reports for any period. Your entries are stored on your phone, and you can optionally back them up to your own Google Drive.

**Platforms:** Android and iPhone (iOS). Google Drive backup is available on Android; Google sign-in for iPhone is being added.

The app has no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no server. Entries stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive.
